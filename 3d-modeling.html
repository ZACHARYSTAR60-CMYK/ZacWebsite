Skull Model

A 3D model of a skull, made for a digital sculpting class.

As part of a digital sculpting class, we were tasked with making a "traditional sculpt." I decided on a skull — after working through Photoshop reference sheets and building the form in ZBrush, I completed the model. Next step is prepping it for 3D printing.
